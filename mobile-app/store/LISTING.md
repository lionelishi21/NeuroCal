## Description (both stores)

The habits that help you lose weight are the same ones that sharpen your brain. NeuroCal puts them in one place and turns them into one number each morning: your Focus Score.

**One score, one next step**
Your Focus Score comes from six things: last night's sleep, evening timing, what you ate, how you feel, how you moved and your surroundings. Under it, Up next shows the one thing that will help most right now, with a button that does it.

**Watch both goals move**
Your plan follows your first three weeks: your Focus Score heading for its target, and your weight trend heading for yours, smoothed so a salty dinner doesn't look like a setback.

**Food without the guesswork**
- Snap a meal and NeuroCal reads it: calories, protein, carbs and fat.
- Scan a package: how processed it is, the additives in it, added sugar and salt, and a cleaner choice, or the same food made at home from your pantry.
- Eating out: menu items from major chains, with a better pick at the same place.
- Meal plans, recipes from what's in your kitchen, and a shopping list review.

**Fast your way**
A fasting timer that follows your meals, or plan a fast until a time. Water, black coffee and plain tea don't break it.

**Coffee, done right**
Check whether your coffee is tested for mould toxins, make Bulletproof coffee, and know your caffeine curfew.

**Your brain, your body**
- Check in in ten seconds: tap how you feel.
- A two-minute Brain speed check, compared with your own usual.
- Sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect, logged without typing.
- Guided movement, like a five-minute calf pump at your desk.
- Healthspan: the weekly habits behind a long, healthy life.
- Your supplement stack, with checks across everything you take.

**Built around you**
Tell NeuroCal about health conditions and it keeps your plan safe: gentler fasting, no weight-loss goal where it isn't right, and supplement interactions flagged.

NeuroCal is a wellness app, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything; check changes to your diet, fasting or supplements with your doctor.

**Subscription:** NeuroCal Pro is $59.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, or $8.00 a month. Cancel any time in your store account settings; you won't be charged if you cancel during the trial.
